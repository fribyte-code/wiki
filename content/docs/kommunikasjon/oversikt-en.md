## [Migadu](https://admin.migadu.com/)

friByte hosts its email with Migadu. We have a nonprofit license there and pay
half price for the Mini plan, i.e. $45 per year.

To reduce spam, add email domains that match spam under
`Email Domains -> @fribyte.no -> Spam Filter -> Sender Denylist`, or click the
following link:
https://admin.migadu.com/domains/79954/spam/sender_denylist. Here you can add
wildcard domains such as `*@*.ru` to block all email from Russian domains. If a
domain is rarely used, but legitimate email may still come from it, you should
add it to the junklist instead of the Denylist so it ends up in spam instead of
being rejected.
